Procedure

Biofilms were grown for 18 hours, treated with a range of phage T4 doses, then tested against an ampicillin dilution series.

Step Procedure Image
1 Grow the liquid culture
  • Start from the E. coli B agar slant
  • Add 5–10 mL sterile LB broth to a culture tube
  • Transfer a colony using an inoculating loop
  • Incubate at 37 °C, 200 rpm until visibly turbid
  • Adjust to an OD600 of 0.10–0.12
2 Pour the Mueller-Hinton plates
  • Dissolve 38 g of Mueller-Hinton agar per liter of water
  • Autoclave at 121 °C for 30 minutes, then cool to ~50 °C
  • Pour 25 mL into each pre-labeled plate
  • Solidify, then store inverted at 4 °C
3 Inoculate and spread the biofilm
  • Vortex the culture for 15 seconds
  • Pipette 100 µL onto the center of each plate
  • Spread evenly with a sterile spreader
  • Leave the lids ajar for 10 minutes to absorb
4 Mature the biofilm
  • Parafilm and invert the plates
  • Incubate statically at 37 °C for 18 hours
  • Allow the biofilm to fully form and mature
5 Expose the biofilms to phage T4
  • Prepare serial dilutions in sterile LB broth
  • Use 0, 104, 106, 108, and 1010 PFU/mL
  • Apply 100 µL of each dilution to a biofilm
  • Incubate statically at 37 °C for 4 hours
6 Collect and standardize
  • Add 1 mL sterile saline and scrape the plate
  • Transfer the sample to a labeled microcentrifuge tube
  • Mix thoroughly to resuspend the cells
  • Match each sample to a 0.5 McFarland standard
7 Spot and read the MIC
  • Prepare ampicillin plates at 0, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 µg/mL
  • Spot 10 µL of each sample and let it dry
  • Incubate inverted at 37 °C for 18 hours
  • Read the MIC: lowest concentration with no visible growth
